What Type of Traveller Are You?

About 61% of people say travelling is their hobby. Complete the quiz in two steps!

STEP 1 Fill in the -ing forms
STEP 2 Choose your answers

💡 Fill in the gaps with the correct -ing form of the verb in brackets. Check your answers, then move to Step 2.

1

A) I enjoy reading by the sea.
B) I love new people.
C) I hate doing nothing.

Slide 8 · Speaking & Writing

What Do They Like?

Look at the characters and write what they might like, love, hate, or enjoy doing. Then work in pairs and compare your answers.

💡 Use: love / like / enjoy / hate / don't like + verb-ing
Example: Chef Sarah loves cooking delicious food. She hates washing up after a long day.
Example:
Chef Sarah loves cooking delicious food for other people. She enjoys baking fresh croissants in the morning. She likes working in a clean kitchen. Sarah doesn't like waiting for people who are late. She hates washing up after a long day of cooking!
