Grammar
Going to

We use the present form of the verb be + going to + verb to talk about the future – and especially the plans we have.

I'm going to go running by the river later.

She's not going to have time today.

What about tonight? Are you going to be busy then?

🇺🇦 be (am / is / are) + going to + дієслово = мої плани на майбутнє.
